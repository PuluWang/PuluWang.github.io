About us / 关于我们

Independent by design.

Pulu Network is the publishing brand of our legally registered independent game company. Our two-person team develops, publishes, and operates its own mobile games on Google Play and the App Store.

Pulu Network 是我们依法注册成立的独立游戏公司所运营的发行品牌。两名团队成员共同负责自有移动游戏的开发、发行与长期运营。

Google Ads API use / 使用说明

Private reporting,
for our own games.

We are developing a read-only internal reporting tool used exclusively by our two team members and only with Google Ads accounts owned or managed by our company.

Internal use only Reporting only No third-party access
01Our ad accountsCompany-owned or authorized Google Ads accounts
02Google Ads APIAggregated, read-only reporting data

The tool is not available to external users, does not manage third-party accounts, and does not sell or share Google Ads data. It does not create customer accounts or modify campaigns, ads, bids, or budgets.
