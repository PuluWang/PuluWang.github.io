2. Information we collect

The exact information may vary by Game, device, platform, SDK configuration, consent choice, and the features you use. We may collect or receive the following categories:

Gameplay and app activity

Game sessions, levels, progress, scores, achievements, game events, feature interactions, settings and preferences, session duration, and ad interactions.

Device and app information

Device model, operating system and version, language and locale, app/package name and version, SDK version, network information, and general device settings.

Identifiers

Advertising identifiers such as Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA where available and permitted, app instance identifiers, Firebase Installation ID, vendor identifiers, and similar identifiers.

Advertising and attribution

Ad impressions, clicks, video views, campaign and referral source, installs, re-engagement, conversions, and attribution results.

Diagnostics and performance

Crash reports, stack traces, error logs, app launch and performance information, device state, and other technical diagnostic data.

Approximate location and network data

IP address and an approximate country or region inferred from it. We do not use this information to collect precise GPS location under this policy.

Purchase events

Product identifier, purchase time, transaction status, and related in-app purchase events. Payment credentials are processed by Google Play or Apple; we do not receive your complete card or payment account details.

Some game progress and preferences may also be stored locally on your device.

7. Data retention and deletion requests

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including game operation, analytics, advertising measurement, security, dispute resolution, and legal compliance. Retention periods vary by data type, Game, SDK configuration, and service provider. Aggregated or de-identified information may be retained where it can no longer reasonably identify you.

Our Games do not create user accounts, so there is no in-app account deletion function or in-app data deletion function. Local game data may remain on your device until you clear the Game’s storage or uninstall the Game. You may email 116779363@qq.com to make a privacy or deletion request. We will assess and respond as required by applicable law.

Because most analytics and advertising data is associated with device or app identifiers rather than a name or account, we may need information such as the Game name, platform, device identifier, or advertising identifier to locate a record. In some cases we may be unable to identify or delete a particular record, especially if it has been aggregated or de-identified. Data held by third-party providers is also subject to their retention and deletion procedures; where appropriate, we may direct or transmit a request to the relevant provider.
