1. Company and business model

Legal entity and tool operator Shanghai Pulu Network Technology Co., Ltd. 上海噗噜网络科技有限公司 · Brand: Pulu Network · Founded in 2020

Pulu Network is the publishing brand operated by Shanghai Pulu Network Technology Co., Ltd., a legally registered independent mobile game company based in Shanghai, China. Our two-person team develops, publishes, and operates its own games on Google Play and the Apple App Store.

Our business is supported by in-app advertising and in-app purchases. We use Google Ads only to promote and evaluate our own games. We are not an advertising agency and do not provide advertising management services to third-party clients.

2. Google Ads API use case

We are developing a private, internal reporting tool used exclusively by our two team members. The tool uses the Google Ads API only with Google Ads accounts owned by or authorized for management by Shanghai Pulu Network Technology Co., Ltd.

01

Retrieve reports

Retrieve aggregated metrics such as impressions, clicks, advertising cost, conversions, and campaign or ad group performance.

02

Evaluate performance

Create internal reports to evaluate user-acquisition performance and return on advertising spend for our games.

03

Reduce manual work

Reduce repetitive manual report downloads from the Google Ads interface and organize results for internal review.

4. Users, access, and limitations

  • The tool is available only to our two authorized team members and is not offered to external users.
  • It does not manage third-party advertising accounts or create accounts for customers.
  • It does not create or modify campaigns, ads, keywords, bids, budgets, targeting, or other Google Ads settings.
  • It does not sell, license, or share Google Ads data with external users or third-party clients.
  • Data is used only for internal reporting and decisions concerning our own games.

5. Data handling and security

The tool retrieves aggregated reporting data required for internal reports, including campaign and ad group identifiers or names, impressions, clicks, cost, conversions, dates, and related performance metrics. Access is restricted to authorized team members.

Developer tokens, OAuth credentials, customer identifiers, and other sensitive account information are never published on this website or made available to external users. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect access credentials and reporting data.

1. 公司与业务模式

公司主体及工具运营方 上海噗噜网络科技有限公司 Shanghai Pulu Network Technology Co., Ltd. · 品牌：Pulu Network · 成立于2020年

Pulu Network 是上海噗噜网络科技有限公司运营的游戏品牌。我们是一家位于中国上海、依法注册成立的独立移动游戏公司。两名团队成员共同负责自有游戏在 Google Play 和 Apple App Store 的开发、发行与长期运营。

我们的业务主要通过应用内广告和应用内购买获得收入。我们仅使用 Google Ads 推广和评估自有游戏，不是广告代理商，也不为第三方客户提供广告管理服务。

2. Google Ads API 使用场景

我们正在开发一款仅供两名团队成员使用的私有内部报告工具。该工具只通过 Google Ads API 访问上海噗噜网络科技有限公司拥有或经授权管理的 Google Ads 账户。

01

读取报告

读取展示次数、点击次数、广告费用、转化以及广告系列或广告组表现等汇总指标。

02

评估推广效果

生成内部报告，用于评估自有游戏的用户获取表现和广告投入回报。

03

减少重复操作

减少从 Google Ads 界面重复手动下载报告的工作，并整理结果供团队内部查看。

4. 用户、访问权限与功能限制

  • 该工具仅供两名获得授权的团队成员使用，不向外部用户开放。
  • 该工具不管理第三方广告账户，也不为客户创建账户。
  • 该工具不会创建或修改广告系列、广告、关键字、出价、预算、定位或其他 Google Ads 设置。
  • 我们不会向外部用户或第三方客户出售、许可或共享 Google Ads 数据。
  • 相关数据只用于自有游戏的内部报告和经营决策。

5. 数据处理与安全

该工具只读取生成内部报告所需的汇总数据，包括广告系列及广告组标识符或名称、展示、点击、费用、转化、日期及相关表现指标。访问权限仅限获得授权的团队成员。

开发者令牌、OAuth 凭据、客户标识符及其他敏感账户信息绝不会发布在本网站上，也不会提供给外部用户。我们采取合理的技术和组织措施保护访问凭据及报告数据。
