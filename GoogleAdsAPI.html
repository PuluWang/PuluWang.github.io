1. Company and business model

Pulu Network is the publishing brand operated by Shanghai Pulu Network Technology Co., Ltd., a legally registered independent mobile game company based in Shanghai, China. Our two-person team develops, publishes, and operates its own games on Google Play and the Apple App Store.

Our business is supported by in-app advertising and in-app purchases. We use Google Ads only to promote and evaluate our own games. We are not an advertising agency and do not provide advertising management services to third-party clients.

2. Google Ads API use case and account structure

We are developing a private, internal reporting, ad-group, and creative-management tool used exclusively by our two team members. It creates or updates ad groups only inside existing, authorized Pulu campaigns and never creates campaigns. The Google Ads manager account (MCC) and developer token are owned and operated by Shanghai Pulu Network Technology Co., Ltd.

The tool accesses a separate, dedicated Google Ads advertiser account owned and managed by GatherOne, our authorized advertising agency. This account is used exclusively to advertise mobile games owned and operated by Pulu Network and is accessible through our company-owned manager account with GatherOne's authorization.

01

Retrieve reports

Retrieve aggregated metrics such as impressions, clicks, advertising cost, conversions, and campaign or ad group performance.

02

Create and maintain ad groups

Create or update ad groups only inside existing, authorized Pulu campaigns. The tool never creates campaigns.

03

Replace creative

Replace underperforming image, video, text, and HTML5/H5 creative with pre-approved assets for the same Pulu game.

4. Controlled ad-group and creative operations

Within existing, allowlisted Pulu campaigns, the tool can create ad groups and update ad-group resources, but it never creates campaigns. When an authorized team member confirms that creative is underperforming, or when Google Ads provides an ad-strength, policy, or serving signal that requires replacement, the tool can create or attach a pre-approved replacement image, video, text asset, or HTML5/H5 bundle for the same game.

The tool validates the planned API operation before execution and records the result. A superseded resource is paused or removed only after its approved replacement succeeds and only where the Google Ads resource type supports that operation.

5. Users, access, and limitations

  • The tool is available only to our two authorized team members and is not offered to external users.
  • It does not access GatherOne's other client accounts or unrelated campaigns, and it does not create accounts for customers.
  • Mutations are limited to authorized ad groups and pre-approved creative assets inside existing, allowlisted Pulu campaigns.
  • It does not create campaigns or change campaign or account budgets, bids, billing, account ownership, audiences, or conversion actions.
  • It does not sell, license, or share Google Ads data with external users or third-party clients.
  • Data and mutation results are used only for internal reporting, ad-group management, and creative replacement concerning our own games.

6. Data handling and security

The tool retrieves aggregated reporting data only from the dedicated GatherOne-managed account used for Pulu campaigns. The data required for internal reports includes campaign and ad group identifiers or names, impressions, clicks, cost, conversions, dates, and related performance metrics. For controlled changes, it records the affected resource identifiers, the approved replacement, and the API result. Access is restricted to authorized Pulu team members.

Developer tokens, OAuth credentials, customer identifiers, and other sensitive account information are never published on this website or made available to external users. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect access credentials and reporting data.

1. 公司与业务模式

公司主体及工具运营方 上海噗噜网络科技有限公司 Shanghai Pulu Network Technology Co., Ltd. · 品牌：Pulu Network · 成立于2020年

Pulu Network 是上海噗噜网络科技有限公司运营的游戏品牌。我们是一家位于中国上海、依法注册成立的独立移动游戏公司。两名团队成员共同负责自有游戏在 Google Play 和 Apple App Store 的开发、发行与长期运营。

我们的业务主要通过应用内广告和应用内购买获得收入。我们仅使用 Google Ads 推广和评估自有游戏，不是广告代理商，也不为第三方客户提供广告管理服务。

2. Google Ads API 使用场景与账户结构

我们正在开发一款仅供两名团队成员使用的私有内部报告、广告组与素材管理工具。工具只在现有且经过授权的 Pulu 广告系列内创建或更新广告组，绝不会创建广告系列。Google Ads 经理账户（MCC）和开发者令牌由上海噗噜网络科技有限公司拥有并运营。

该工具访问一个由我们的授权广告代理商 GatherOne 所有并管理的独立 Google Ads 广告账户。该账户仅用于推广 Pulu Network 自主开发和运营的移动游戏，并经 GatherOne 授权，通过本公司自有的经理账户访问。

01

读取报告

读取展示次数、点击次数、广告费用、转化以及广告系列或广告组表现等汇总指标。

02

创建和维护广告组

只在现有且经过授权的 Pulu 广告系列内创建或更新广告组，绝不会创建广告系列。

03

替换创意

使用同一 Pulu 游戏预先批准的素材，替换表现不佳的图片、视频、文本和 HTML5/H5 创意。

3. 内部工作流程

→

4. 受控的广告组与素材操作

在现有且列入白名单的 Pulu 广告系列内，该工具可以创建广告组并更新广告组资源，但绝不会创建广告系列。当获得授权的团队成员确认创意表现不佳，或 Google Ads 提供需要替换的广告效力、政策或投放状态信号时，工具可以为同一游戏创建或关联预先批准的替换图片、视频、文本素材或 HTML5/H5 素材包。

工具会在执行前验证计划中的 API 操作并记录结果。只有在已批准的替换素材成功后，并且相应 Google Ads 资源类型支持该操作时，才会暂停或移除被替换的资源。

5. 用户、访问权限与功能限制

  • 该工具仅供两名获得授权的团队成员使用，不向外部用户开放。
  • 该工具不会访问 GatherOne 其他客户的账户或无关广告系列，也不为客户创建账户。
  • 修改操作仅限现有白名单 Pulu 广告系列内经过授权的广告组和预先批准的创意素材。
  • 该工具不会创建广告系列，也不会修改广告系列或账户预算、出价、结算、账户所有权、受众或转化操作。
  • 我们不会向外部用户或第三方客户出售、许可或共享 Google Ads 数据。
  • 相关数据和修改结果只用于自有游戏的内部报告、广告组管理与素材替换。

6. 数据处理与安全

该工具仅从 GatherOne 为 Pulu 广告系列管理的独立账户读取汇总报告数据。内部报告所需数据包括广告系列及广告组标识符或名称、展示、点击、费用、转化、日期及相关表现指标。执行受控修改时，工具会记录受影响的资源标识符、批准的替换素材和 API 结果。访问权限仅限获得授权的 Pulu 团队成员。

开发者令牌、OAuth 凭据、客户标识符及其他敏感账户信息绝不会发布在本网站上，也不会提供给外部用户。我们采取合理的技术和组织措施保护访问凭据及报告数据。
